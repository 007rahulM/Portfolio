Animations should explain intent, not steal attention. In this portfolio I only animate what needs focus — headings, menus, and the first impression.

## My rules
1. Fade and rise for primary headers.
2. Animate menus once, then let them be.
3. Keep durations short and easing soft.

## What I’m learning
- `whileInView` for section reveals
- `AnimatePresence` for mobile navigation
- Layout animations for smoother transitions
