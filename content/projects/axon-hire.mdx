Axon Hire is a full-stack hiring platform that combines smart resume parsing with an intuitive recruiter pipeline. The goal is to remove friction for both candidates and hiring teams while keeping the system scalable.

Key focus areas:
- Multi-model AI scoring for candidate ranking
- Secure file uploads for resumes and avatars
- Role-based workflows for recruiters, job seekers, and admins
